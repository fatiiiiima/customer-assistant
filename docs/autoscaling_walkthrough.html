How to read the arrows. The number on an arrow is the step in the list above. The arrowhead points to where the information goes. A solid arrow means the sender starts it: it sends, or it calls. A dashed arrow means the receiver asked for it: it reads or pulls. The words on the arrow say what travels. The number is the first step that uses the arrow: the report to Redis (3) is also part of steps 5, 11 and 12, and the Kubernetes arrow (10) is also used in step 12.
