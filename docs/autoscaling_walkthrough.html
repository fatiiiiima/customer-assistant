Part 2: a burst of callers, minute by minute

Callers wanting a call rise from 100 to 700 within two minutes, stay there for five minutes, then fall to 150 (the chart is capped at a ratio of 4, and the first minutes go higher). The autoscaler follows the rules in deploy/k8s/voice.yaml: target 0.7, between 8 and 72 pods, at most double every 15 seconds going up, 2 pods a minute going down after 5 quiet minutes. A pod takes 90 seconds to load its speech models and start taking calls (an assumed figure: the launcher says "a minute or two"). This is an illustration of the rules, not a measurement of a real cluster.
