A teaching version of the scaling rule in deploy/k8s/voice.yaml (scale on demand at 70% of capacity, minimum 8, double at most every 15 s, wait 5 minutes then remove at most 2 a minute). The 45-second worker start, the 3-minute average call and the caller curve are invented for the demo; nothing here is measured.
