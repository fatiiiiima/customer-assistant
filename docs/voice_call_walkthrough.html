The whole call

  1. The page asks the router for a worker once, then opens a WebSocket straight to it. It is one small request, not on the path of any turn.
  2. The worker greets after 3 s of quiet, or answers if you speak first. The opening line is about 2 s of speech.
  3. Every turn follows the animation above. Behind it the call is saved to Redis.
  4. If you stop talking: "are you still there?" after 8 s, and the call ends 7 s later. It is kept: tap the microphone and it carries on.
  5. A goodbye or a hand-off ends the call and keeps the conversation; opening it again, on any worker, picks up where you left off, until you close the tab.

Medians of 192 turns on 3 November: one worker on an M3 Pro, five conversations in three Mac voices, 1 to 8 callers, Dubai to Bedrock in us-east-1. The medians of the steps add up to about 1.0 s for turns where the model speaks first, but those turns measured 1.2 s at the median because slow steps (a held turn's wait, a busy voice server) come in the same turns. The turn where the model must decide first is a small group (14 turns). Scenarios use the step medians; the model's request times are the measured medians relative to the arrival of the words.
